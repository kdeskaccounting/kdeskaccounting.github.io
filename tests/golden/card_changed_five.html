What changed this week

Walt Disney World · week of 2026-09-14

  • Test Track
    Reopened on Tuesday after a long refurbishment, and it is already the busiest ride at EPCOT.
  • Space Mountain
    Goes down for refurbishment on Monday and is scheduled to stay closed until early spring.
  • Jungle Cruise
    Held a steady thirty minute wait all week, the flattest line in the Magic Kingdom right now.
  • Rise of the Resistance
    Posted the longest wait of the week on Saturday, and it has not dropped below an hour since.
  • Haunted Mansion
    Switched to its holiday overlay on Friday, which pushed the evening wait up by about twenty minutes.
