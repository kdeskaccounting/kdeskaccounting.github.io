## Post text

The month-1 operating lease entry has two different numbers in it — the expense and the liability reduction — and most spreadsheets get one of them wrong.

Here is a 4-minute walkthrough of the workbook I built to produce both, for 20 leases, 120 months, with a reconciliation that reads $0 before anything posts.

What's in it:
• Lease register: 9 yellow cells per lease, liability and ROU asset calculated at commencement
• 120-month schedule per lease — interest, principal, ROU amortization
• JE Generator with your GL codes, debits = credits check
• Rollforward split current / long-term, maturity analysis for the footnote
• Reconciliation: JE Generator vs. register, every variance $0

Pure Excel. No macros. Works on Windows and Mac.
