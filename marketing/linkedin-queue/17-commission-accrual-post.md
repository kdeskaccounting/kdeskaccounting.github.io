Most SaaS closes book commissions as one entry. It's two: the accrual when the rep earns it, and the ASC 340-40 capitalization of the same dollars as an asset. Mix them up and a cancelled deal clears the liability while the asset keeps amortizing a commission nobody was ever paid.

New post: one September cohort booked end to end — accrual, capitalization, the clawback when a deal falls through, payroll taxes, payout, and the three-line reconciliation that proves the asset and the expense add back to cash.
