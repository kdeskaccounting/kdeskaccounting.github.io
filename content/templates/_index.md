Seven Excel workbooks built for controllers and finance managers at Series A–C SaaS companies — plus one for the equity-compensated employees they work with.

Each template is purpose-built for one workflow. No generic spreadsheet packs. Every workbook ships formula-complete with sample data and yellow input cells — and every paid workbook except the RSU Tax Planner has a free, reduced-capacity version so you can see the mechanics on your own data before you buy (the RSU planner has a [free calculator](/rsu-tax-calculator/) instead).

## Available Templates

### [SaaS Controller Bundle](/templates/bundle/) — $599
The five SaaS controller workbooks plus the free close checklist in one purchase. $693 bought separately. Same Setup tab and yellow input cells in every file; the three accounting workbooks (leases, commissions, fixed assets) each add a JE Generator, a rollforward and a reconciliation tab.

### [ASC 842 Lease Accounting Workbook](/templates/asc842/) — $249
ROU asset, lease liability, amortization schedule, journal entries, rollforward, and disclosure table for operating and finance leases. 20-lease capacity. Escalating rent, free rent and irregular payment schedules. Free 3-lease version available.

### [ASC 606 Commission Capitalization Workbook](/templates/asc606/) — $249
Capitalizes and amortizes sales commissions under ASC 340-40 for SaaS companies with multi-year contracts. 50-deal capacity, three amortization methods, cancellations and clawbacks, automated journal entries, rollforward and reconciliation. Free 5-deal version available.

### [Fixed Asset Rollforward Workbook](/templates/fixed-assets/) — $79
50-asset register, four depreciation methods (SL, DDB, SYD, units of production), JE generator with QuickBooks / NetSuite / Sage Intacct / Xero presets, disposal log, and a reconciliation tab that ties to your GL balances. Free 5-asset version available.

### [SaaS Metrics & ARR Dashboard](/templates/saas-metrics/) — $67
MRR, ARR, Net New MRR, gross churn, NRR, ARPA, CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC, CAC payback, and Magic Number from seven monthly inputs. 24-month capacity, seven-card board-ready dashboard. Free 6-month version available.

### [Startup Runway Calculator](/templates/runway/) — $49
12 to 48-month cash flow forecast with Base, Optimistic, and Pessimistic scenario modeling. KPI dashboard (runway, cash-zero date, breakeven, capital required), five input tabs, two-rate revenue growth, and frequency logic per line item. Free 12-month version available.

### [RSU Tax Planner](/templates/rsu-planner/) — $149
Every RSU grant and vest through 2030: the gap between the 22% payroll withholds and your marginal rate, per vest and per year; the federal safe-harbor amount as four equal quarterly installments; a per-paycheck W-4 equivalent; shares sold to cover; the $1M supplemental line. Personal-finance companion to the free [RSU tax calculator](/rsu-tax-calculator/).

### [Month-End Close Checklist + Tie-Out Workbook](/templates/month-end-close/) — Free
42-task close calendar across 5 phases, 18 subledger-to-GL reconciliations with a tie-out tolerance flag, 50-row JE tracker that checks every entry balances, and a printable sign-off page. Pay what you want, $0 minimum.
